Handcrafted luxury gifts from India

Mukul Goyal's gifting collection brings together sculptural brass objects designed for everyday use – pen holders, photo frames, vases, tea light holders, serveware, barware and jewellery. Each piece is designed in the studio and finished by hand, making it a gift that feels personal rather than picked off a shelf.

For businesses, our corporate gifting service helps HR teams, founders and event planners source premium gifts in volume – for Diwali, client appreciation, employee milestones, weddings and conferences.
